Contact & Refunds

Reach us

Email: shiftly.campus [at] gmail [dot] com
We answer within 72 hours. Include your Shiftly username.

Gig disputes

Send the gig ID and a screenshot of the in-app chat. We review and can release or refund the held escrow.

Payment refunds

  • Failed UPI/card payments — if money left your account but the wallet did not update, the amount auto-refunds to the source via Razorpay in 5–7 business days. Quote your UPI reference number when you write to us.
  • Escrow refunds — cancelled OPEN gigs refund instantly and automatically back to your wallet.
  • Duplicate top-ups — both payments credit your wallet; write to us and we will refund the duplicate to source.

Account deletion

Email us from your sign-in email address or mention your verified mobile number, and we will delete the account and its data within 7 days (see Privacy Policy).
