Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Shiftly is a campus gig marketplace. This policy explains what we collect and why, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Account details — name, course/year, hostel, and either your Google email (when you use Google sign-in) or your mobile number (when you use OTP sign-in).
  • Profile photo, if you choose to upload one.
  • Marketplace activity — gigs you post or accept, messages you send inside the app, ratings, and wallet transactions.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show your gigs and public profile to other signed-in students, deliver messages, and settle escrow payouts.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your UPI PIN, card number, or bank details — those go directly between you and Razorpay/your bank. We store only the payment reference IDs needed to reconcile your wallet.
  • Sign-in is verified by Google (Firebase Authentication). We receive only your verified email address.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your personal data.
  • Show ads or use third-party ad trackers.
  • Expose your wallet balance, escrow, UPI ID, email or mobile number to other users — public profiles show only name, role, course, hostel, photo and rating.

Your controls

  • Edit or remove your photo and profile details any time from Profile → Edit Profile.
  • To delete your account and data, contact us (see Contact page) and we will remove it within 7 days, except settlement records we must keep for accounting.

Data storage

Data is stored on the server running this deployment. Sessions use signed tokens stored in your browser's local storage; signing out clears them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and a notice will appear in the app.
