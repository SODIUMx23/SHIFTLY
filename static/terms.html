By using Shiftly you agree to these terms. They exist to keep a student marketplace fair and safe.

1. What Shiftly is

A campus marketplace where students post small jobs ("gigs") — food runs, tutoring, lifting, errands — and other students complete them for the posted bounty.

2. Escrow & payments

  • Posting a gig locks the reward amount into escrow immediately.
  • When the poster marks the gig complete, 97% of the reward is paid to the worker's wallet instantly; 3% is the platform fee.
  • If an OPEN gig is cancelled before anyone accepts it, the full escrow returns to the poster.
  • Wallet top-ups are processed by Razorpay under their terms. Refunds for failed payments follow Razorpay/UPI timelines (typically 5–7 business days).

3. Rules of the bazaar

  • No illegal work, alcohol, drugs, plagiarism-for-hire, or anything against your institution's rules. Offending gigs are removed and balances may be frozen.
  • One account per person. Identity is tied to your Google account or mobile number.
  • Complete what you accept. Repeated no-shows lead to account suspension.

4. Disputes

If work is delivered but not marked complete, or marked complete without delivery, contact us (see Contact page) with the gig ID and chat history. We review within 72 hours and may release or refund the escrow at our discretion.

5. Liability

Gigs are contracts directly between the two students involved. Shiftly provides the listing, messaging and escrow rails and is not a party to the work itself. Use common sense: meet in public campus spaces.

Continued use after an update to these terms means you accept the new version.
